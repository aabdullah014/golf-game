This is the multiplayer card game GOLF.

The objective of the game is to have the lowest score at the end of the game.
The score is determined by the face value of the cards you hold. Jack = 11, Queen = 12, Black King = 13, Red King = -1. So the Red King is typically the most desirable card in the game as it is the only card that by itself can lower a player's score.
Additionally, there are special Action cards. These are as follows:
7,8 -> you can look at one of your own cards.
9, 10 -> you can look at one of another player's cards.
Jack -> you can perform a "blind swap"; you can swap one of your cards with one of another player's cards without looking at either.
Queen -> you can perform a "look and swap" you can look at one of another player's cards, as well as swap. This can be the same card.

The game is over when a player calls GOLF. The round will then complete after all players play and the lowest score will win. A player must call GOLF at the start of their turn. They cannot make a move or draw prior to or after calling GOLF.

A sample game would be as follows:
Dealer deals four cards to all players. The remaining cards are placed facedown in the middle of all the players.
Each player arranges their cards in a square. They can look at the bottom two cards prior to the game starting BUT THIS IS THEIR ONLY OPPORTUNITY TO DO SO FREELY WITHOUT PLAAYING A 7 or 8!
The game starts by a player drawing a card from the middle deck. They can choose to keep or play the card. If they play it, then it goes into a discard pile and any action associated with the card is executed. If they choose to keep it, they must discard a card from their possession. If the discarded card is an action card, the action is then performed.
The game progresses in this manner, and players can choose to draw from the middle deck or the discard pile.

There is a special twist in this version of the game. At any point in time, a player can "match" the card on the top of the discard pile. If they have a card that is the same face value as the top of the discard pile, they can discard that card, effectively getting rid of a card for free. If they have multiple matches, they can discard multiple cards. Additionally, if they know another player has a match, they can play that match for that player. In that case, the player who played the card would then give one of their cards to the player from whom they took the card to match. However, if a player mistakenly plays a match, they must not only take the failed match card, but also draw an additional card from the middle deck.
